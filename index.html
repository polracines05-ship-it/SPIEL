System Verification & Check-In Steps OS Specific
Windows
Please open your Task Manager (Press Ctrl + Shift + Esc) then scroll down slowly until you reach the end of the list to ensure all background applications are closed.
Mac (macOS)
Please go to Apple menu > System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording, and ensure your browser or exam application is toggled on. Please also close all open background apps via Command + Tab.
Linux
Please open your system monitor or terminal to close any unauthorized background processes or applications before we proceed with your exam release.
General ID & Room Scan
We will need to verify your identity. Please hold your valid government-issued ID up to the camera. Once verified, please perform a slow 360-degree room scan including your desk surface.
Canned Messages & Tactical Spiels Communications
Warning // Talking
Talking or communicating outside of the exam environment is not allowed during your exam, including reading or mouthing questions. Please do not read questions and answers aloud.
Warning // Face View
Please ensure your face remains fully within camera view at all times throughout the examination session.
Escalation // Support
If you encounter persistent technical difficulties, please email support@certiverse.com for further assistance.
Termination // Violation
Unfortunately, the use of prohibited applications/behavior has been detected, which violates our code of conduct. As a result, this exam will now be terminated. Please email support@certiverse.com for escalation.

FAQ // Rescheduling
Q: What should I do if I miss my exam or experience severe technical disconnection?
Candidates who miss their appointment or face unresolvable drop-offs must contact support@certiverse.com to request rescheduling or ticket review.
FAQ // System Requirements
Q: What are the minimum system requirements for taking a Certiverse exam?
A working webcam, microphone, stable high-speed internet connection, and a compatible modern web browser or Secure Exam Browser (SEB) depending on client policy.
FAQ // Identification
Q: What forms of ID are accepted during check-in?
A valid government-issued photo ID (Passport, Driver's License, or National ID) that clearly displays your full legal name matching your exam reservation.
