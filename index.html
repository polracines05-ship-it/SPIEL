Guided Check-In Mode // Standard Operating Procedures
1. Welcome ▼
Welcome Spiel
Hello and welcome! I will be your proctor for this exam. We have a few steps to complete before your exam begins. This time does not count against your exam time limit. Please hold while we prepare your exam. Thank you for waiting.
2. Identification Verification ▼
ID Verification
We will need to verify your identity before we release the exam. Please show the front of your valid identification (ID) to the camera. You may use the camera preview on your screen as a guide to confirm your ID is fully visible.
Blurry ID Spiel
Your ID appears blurry on the screen. Could you please adjust it, so it is clearly visible? You may try improving the lighting, placing it flat on the desk, or holding it still for a moment.
3. System Verification ▼
System Check
[ALL] Please make sure that all running applications are closed and shut down. This includes all browser tabs that are not the exam.
[WINDOWS] Please open your Task Manager then scroll down slowly until you reach the end of the list.
4. Clear Desk ▼
Desk Inspection
Thank you. If there are any items on your desk surface, please remove them now. Ensure all electronic devices are put away and out of reach. This includes phones, smartwatches, headphones, smart glasses, and any other electronic device.
5. Room Scan ▼
Room Scan Procedure
Thank you. We will now scan the room to confirm that all security policies are followed. Please slowly direct your webcam or laptop camera around the room. You will need to capture the entire room and the area on your desk.
6. Exam Release ▼
Release Spiel
Thank you for your cooperation. You may now click the “Hide Video” button on the side of this chat box. Thank you. Do you have any questions before beginning your exam? I will now release your exam.
Canned Messages & Tactical Spiels
Troubleshooting

Warnings
Talking or communicating outside of the exam environment is not allowed during your exam, including reading or mouthing questions. Please do not read the questions and answers aloud.
Termination
Unfortunately, the use of prohibited application has been detected during this exam, which violates our code of conduct. As a result, this exam will now be terminated. Please email support@certiverse.com and they can escalate your issue.
