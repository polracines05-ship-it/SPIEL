3. System Verification [PLATFORM SPECIFIC]

11 Scripts
ALL
Please make sure that all running applications are closed and shut down. This includes all browser tabs that are not the exam.
WINDOWS
Please open your Task Manager then scroll down slowly until you reach the end of the list.
WINDOWS
Please open your System Tray by clicking the arrow icon (^) at the bottom-right corner of your taskbar. Right-click each application and close any that offers a 'Quit' or 'Exit' option.
MAC
Please open your Activity Monitor then scroll down slowly until you reach the end of the list.
MAC
Press Option + Command + Esc simultaneously to open the Force Quit Applications window and ensure all applications not related to the exam are closed.
LINUX
Please open your System Monitor. Please go to the Processes tab and scroll down slowly until you reach the end of the list.
