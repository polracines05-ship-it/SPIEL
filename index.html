    Hardware & System Troubleshooting

    Step-by-step diagnostic workflows for candidate Audio, Camera, Screen Sharing, and SEB issues.

    🎤 Audio Issue — Microphone Not Working (Non-SEB) ➤
    Step 1 — Browser Verification
    Confirm Supported Browser
    Check if candidate is on Chrome or Edge.
    "Before we begin troubleshooting, please confirm that you are using the latest version of Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge."
    Step 2 — Browser Switch Test
    Chrome ↔ Edge Isolation Test
    Instruct candidate to switch alternate browsers.
    "If you are currently using Google Chrome, please try Microsoft Edge instead. If you are using Microsoft Edge, please switch to Google Chrome and test again."
    Step 3 — Microphone Detection Check
    Verify Device Detection
    Confirm OS sees connected input hardware.
    "Please open your system sound settings and confirm that a microphone is listed and detected as an available input device."
    Step 4 — Microphone Selection Check
    Verify Correct Input Device
    Ensure primary input target is chosen.
    "Please check your browser and system audio settings and ensure that the correct microphone is selected as your active input device."
    Step 5 — Mute Settings Check
    Physical and Software Mute Verification
    Inspect physical inline buttons and soft mutes.
    "Please check your device settings and ensure that your microphone is not muted."
    Step 6 — Browser Permission Check
    Microphone Access Permission
    Check browser lock popup permissions.
    "Please verify that your browser has permission to access your microphone. If prompted, select 'Allow' when granting microphone access."
    Step 7 — Operating System Permission Check
    System Privacy Settings
    Confirm OS privacy mic toggles are ON.
    "Please verify that your operating system allows applications and websites to access your microphone."
    Step 8 — Close Other Applications
    Application Conflict Check
    Shut down background communication software.
    "Please close any applications that may be using your microphone, such as Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Discord, Webex, OBS Studio, or similar software."
    Step 9 — VPN / Security Restriction Check
    Security Software Review
    Check corporate network blockers or VPNs.
    "Are you currently connected to a VPN or using security software that may restrict browser permissions? If so, please temporarily disconnect the VPN or verify whether the software may be blocking microphone access."
    Step 10 — External Microphone Test
    Platform vs. Hardware Isolation
    Test mic inside Windows/Mac system recorder.
    "Please test your microphone outside of the exam platform using your device's sound settings. Is the microphone working normally there?"
    Step 11 — Company Device Check
    Identify Corporate Restrictions
    Inquire if candidate uses a company machine and offer personal device alternative.
    "May I confirm whether you are using a company-issued device or a personal device? Some company-managed devices may have security restrictions that can affect exam requirements. If you are currently using a company-issued device, please try using a personal device if one is available."
    Step 12 — Rejoin Session
    Refresh Device Detection
    Re-enter session to force device polling.
    "Please leave the session and rejoin from the exam dashboard so the system can re-detect your microphone settings."
    Step 13 — Restart Device
    Full Computer Reboot
    Reboot system to clear media hooks.
    "Please perform a full restart of your device and rejoin the exam session."
    Step 14 — Alternate Device / Escalation
    Secondary Machine or Support Escalation
    Contact support@certiverse.com or use secondary machine.
    "If available, please try using another personal device. If the issue continues, please contact support@certiverse.com for further assistance."

    Platform vs. Hardware Test

    "Please open your device's Camera application and confirm whether your camera functions normally outside of the exam platform."
    Step 11 — Company Device Check
    Corporate Policy Review
    Inquire whether candidate uses corporate laptop and offer personal device alternative.
    "May I confirm whether you are using a company-issued device or a personal device? Some company-managed devices may have security restrictions that can affect exam requirements. If you are currently using a company-issued device, please try using a personal device if one is available."
    Step 12 — Rejoin Session
    Relaunch Exam Session
    Re-enter session from dashboard.
    "Please leave the session and rejoin from the exam dashboard so the system can re-detect your camera settings."
    Step 13 — Restart Device
    Full Computer Reboot
    Reboot system to reset media capture drivers.
    "Please restart your device and rejoin the exam session."
    Step 14 — Alternate Device / Escalation
    Secondary Machine or Support Escalation
    Contact support@certiverse.com or switch devices.
    "If available, please try using another personal device. If the issue continues, please contact support@certiverse.com for further assistance."

    Confirm Supported Browser

    Unplug secondary monitors.
    "Are you currently using more than one monitor or display? If so, please disconnect all additional monitors and use only one monitor during the exam."
    Step 4 — Close Remote Access Applications
    Close Remote Desktop Software
    Close AnyDesk, TeamViewer, Citrix, VMware, Parsec.
    "Please close any remote access software such as AnyDesk, TeamViewer, Citrix Workspace, VMware, Remote Desktop, Parsec, or similar applications."
    Step 5 — Entire Screen Selection
    Select Full Desktop View
    Ensure candidate selects 'Entire Screen' tab.
    "When prompted to share your screen, please select 'Entire Screen' rather than a specific browser tab or application window."
    Step 6 — Screen Sharing Permission Check
    Browser & OS Screen Capture Permissions
    Check macOS Privacy Screen Recording settings.
    "Please verify that screen-sharing permissions are enabled within both your browser and operating system settings."
    Step 7 — Device Permission Verification
    Full Hardware Permissions Check
    Confirm mic, camera, and display access.
    "Please verify that your microphone, camera, and screen-sharing permissions are fully enabled on your device."
    Step 8 — VPN / Security Restriction Check
    Inspect Security Blockers
    Disconnect active VPN connections.
    "Are you currently connected to a VPN or using security software that may restrict screen sharing? If so, please temporarily disconnect the VPN or verify whether the software may be blocking screen-sharing access."

    Confirm if device is managed by company policies and offer personal device alternative.

    Step 10 — Rejoin Session
    Close & Relaunch Session
    Relaunch exam from candidate dashboard.
    "Please close the session and launch it again from the exam dashboard."
    Step 11 — Restart Device
    Full System Restart
    Reboot machine to clear display stack.
    "Please restart your device and attempt screen sharing again after rejoining the exam."
    Step 12 — Network Isolation Test
    Switch Connection / Hotspot
    Connect to mobile hotspot or secondary Wi-Fi.
    "If possible, please switch to an alternate internet connection, such as a mobile hotspot or a different Wi‑Fi network."
    Step 13 — Alternate Device / Escalation
    Secondary Hardware or Support Ticket
    Contact support@certiverse.com or switch devices.
    "If available, please try using another personal device. If the issue continues, please contact support@certiverse.com for additional assistance."

    🛠️ Technical Requirements & Troubleshooting

    Does my computer meet the necessary requirements?
    Candidates can access the Certiverse System Requirements & Hardware Guide to check the pre-flight requirements. It is highly recommended to run the specific compatibility tool provided for your exam ahead of time.
    "Please verify that your computer meets all system requirements by using the pre-exam compatibility tool on your Certiverse dashboard. It is highly recommended to run the compatibility tool provided for your exam ahead of time."
    Am I able to use a Chromebook?
    No, the test environment does not support Chromebooks. You must use a device that meets the full hardware specifications.
    "No, the test environment does not support Chromebooks. You must use a desktop or laptop device that meets full system requirements."
    Why do I see a blank page when navigating to Certiverse.com?
    Certain corporate networks or VPNs block the platform. Candidates experiencing this should switch to a personal machine and connection.
    "Certain corporate networks or VPNs block the platform. If you see a blank page, please switch to a personal machine and personal network connection."
    What should I do if my camera isn't working?
    Check for a physical camera switch or privacy button on your device. Ensure it is toggled to the "on" position before launching the session so the platform can detect it.
    "Please check for a physical camera switch or privacy button on your device and ensure it is toggled to the 'on' position before launching the session."

    📝 Booking, Vouchers, & Exam Management

    What are voucher codes and how do I use them?
    Vouchers are prepaid codes issued by authorized Test Sponsors. They allow you to pay for and schedule your exam directly through the portal.
    "Vouchers are prepaid codes issued by authorized Test Sponsors. You can enter your voucher code at checkout to pay for and schedule your exam."
    Where can I manage my scheduled exams?
    Once logged in, all activities are centralized in the My Exams dashboard (certiverse.com/#/dashboard). This is where you go to review, launch, cancel, or reschedule your tests.
    "You can manage all scheduled exams directly from your My Exams dashboard at certiverse.com/#/dashboard to review, launch, cancel, or reschedule your session."

    🛡️ Online Proctoring & AI Monitoring

    What happens if I get disconnected during the exam?
    If you lose connectivity, you can immediately rejoin the session from your dashboard. Candidates have up to 60 minutes to relaunch an abandoned exam before it autocompletes. If a disconnection exceeds a 30-minute threshold, you must contact support to reschedule.
    "If you get disconnected, you can rejoin directly from your dashboard. You have up to 60 minutes to relaunch before autocompletion, but if disconnected for over 30 minutes, please contact support@certiverse.com."
    How do proctors communicate with candidates?
    Communication is conducted entirely over text chat or email via support@certiverse.com. Support staff can also offer remote control assistance if necessary, but phone support is not available.
    "All proctor communication is conducted over text chat inside the platform or via email at support@certiverse.com. Phone support is not available."
    What triggers an exam pause?
    The AI-assisted proctoring system monitors your webcam. It will automatically pause your test and alert a human proctor if it flags an anomaly, such as your face leaving the camera frame or another person walking into view.
    "The proctoring system monitors your webcam and will automatically pause your test if your face leaves the view frame or another person enters the room."
    What are the core testing rules?
    You must test in a private, well-lit room completely alone. External monitors must be disconnected, and taking screenshots, photos, or copying and pasting text is strictly prohibited.
    "You must test in a private, well-lit room alone. External monitors must be disconnected, and taking screenshots or copying text is strictly prohibited."

    📞 Candidate Support Contact Information

    How do I get in touch for help? What are support hours?
    For account issues, general questions, or registration problems, open a ticket at the Certiverse Support Portal or email support@certiverse.com. Support operates 24/7 with typical responses within 24 hours.
    "For help, open a ticket at the Certiverse Support Portal or email support@certiverse.com. Support operates 24/7 with a typical response time within 24 hours."

    🕒 Scheduling & Timelines

    Booking advance time, grace period, and proctor wait times
    Sessions must be booked 24h in advance (no same-day booking). 30-minute check-in grace period applies. Proctor wait times average 5 to 10 minutes.
    "Exams must be scheduled at least 24 hours in advance. You have a 30-minute grace period from your start time to check in. Proctor connection wait times are typically 5 to 10 minutes."

    🎟️ Voucher & Expiration Rules

    What happens if a voucher expires or an exam is abandoned?
    Expired vouchers or launched/abandoned exams mark the voucher as used and it cannot be reinstated or recycled.
    "Vouchers must be used before their designated expiration date. If an exam is launched and abandoned, the voucher is marked as used and cannot be reinstated."

    🪟 Workspace Setup & Desk Compliance

    Drinks, desk items, and dual-monitor rules
    Drinks permitted only in clear, label-free containers. Desk must be clear within arm's reach. Secondary monitors must be physically disconnected.
    "Drinks are permitted only in clear, label-free containers. Remove all non-essential items within arm's reach, and physically unplug any secondary monitors."

    🌐 System Preferences & Languages

    Can I change the interface language?
    Yes, Certiverse supports Japanese, Spanish, and Simplified Chinese via the top-right language dropdown.
    "You can switch the platform language (including Japanese, Spanish, and Simplified Chinese) using the language dropdown in the upper right-hand corner."
