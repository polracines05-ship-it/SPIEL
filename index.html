    Hardware & System Troubleshooting

    Step-by-step diagnostic workflows for candidate Audio, Screen Sharing, and Camera issues across Non-SEB and SEB environments.

    🔐 NON-SEB (STANDARD BROWSER EXAMS)

    🎤 Audio — Microphone Not Working (Non-SEB)

    Step 1 & 2 — Start & Browser Check
    Verify Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge
    1. Start: Microphone not working.
    2. Using Chrome or Edge? • No → Switch browser. • Yes → Continue to next step.
    "Please check the following before starting the exam:\nUse the latest version of Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge."
    Step 3 — Device Mute Settings
    Mic Muted in Device Settings?
    3. Mic muted in device settings? • Yes → Unmute mic in settings. • No → Continue to next step.
    "Please check for a microphone switch or button on your device or headset and make sure it is unmuted."
    Step 4 & 5 — Browser & OS Permissions
    Browser & OS Microphone Permissions
    4. Browser mic permission enabled? • No → Allow mic in browser. • Yes → Continue.
    5. OS mic permission enabled? • No → Enable in OS settings. • Yes → Continue.
    "Please verify that your browser has permission to access your camera, microphone, and screen. You may see a small lock icon or popup at the top of your browser."
    Step 6 & 7 — Input Device & Corporate VPN
    Select Correct Mic & VPN / Company Device Check
    6. Correct mic selected in browser? • No → Select correct input device. • Yes → Continue.
    7. VPN or company device? • Yes → Disable VPN or use personal machine. • No → Continue.
    "Please try to disable any VPN or ZScaler that might be causing the issue. Also, check your browser and computer settings to confirm camera and screen sharing permissions are enabled."
    Step 8, 9, 10 & 11 — Rejoin, Reboot & Fallback
    Rejoin Session, Reboot Machine, or Alternate Device
    8. Ask candidate to rejoin (leave & re-enter). • Still failing → Continue.
    9. Ask candidate to restart device (full reboot resets audio drivers). • Done → Restart & rejoin. • Still failing → Continue.
    10. Use a different device.
    11. End Goal: Microphone issue resolved ✓
    "I'm no longer able to hear/see you. Please refresh your tab. This will take you back to the initial screen where you can adjust the settings to ensure your mic/screen/camera are properly shared."
    "Please try to reboot your machine and launch the exam again."

    🖥️ Screen Sharing — Can't Share Screen (Non-SEB)

    Step 1 & 2 — Start & Browser Compatibility
    Browser Verification
    1. Start: Can't share screen.
    2. Using Chrome or Edge? • No → Switch browser. • Yes → Continue.
    "Please check the following before starting the exam:\nUse the latest version of Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge."
    Step 3 & 4 — Permissions & Dual Monitors
    OS Screen Permissions & Disconnect Secondary Displays
    3. Browser permissions enabled? • No → Enable in OS settings. • Yes → Continue.
    4. Using dual monitors? • Yes → Disconnect 2nd monitor. • No → Continue.
    "Disconnect any external or extended monitors and use only one screen."
    Step 5 & 6 — VPN & Network Switching
    Disable Corporate VPN / Try Alternate Wi-Fi or Hotspot
    5. VPN or company device? • Yes → Disable VPN or use personal machine. • No → Continue.
    6. Try a different network (switch to hotspot or Wi-Fi). • Yes → Switch to hotspot/Wi-Fi. • Still failing → Continue.
    "Make sure any VPN or proxy connection is turned off."
    "It seems that you are having internet issues. We can cancel this reservation so you can reschedule the exam when you can use a more reliable network."
    Step 7, 8, 9 & 10 — Rejoin, Reboot & Fallback
    Re-enter Session / Full Device Restart / Alternate Hardware
    7. Ask candidate to rejoin (leave and re-enter session). • Still failing → Continue.
    8. Ask candidate to restart device (full reboot clears permission caches). • Done → Restart & rejoin. • Still failing → Continue.
    9. Use a different device.
    10. End Goal: Screen sharing resolved ✓
    "Seems like you are experiencing issues with your session. Can you please close this browser and relaunch your session from the exam dashboard?"

    📷 Video — Camera Not Working (Non-SEB)

    Step 1, 2 & 3 — Physical Switch & Shutter
    Hardware Camera Switch or Slider Privacy Cover
    1. Start: Camera not working.
    2. Using Chrome or Edge? • No → Switch browser.
    3. Camera covered or physically off? • Yes → Uncover or enable camera switch. • No → Continue.
    "Please check for a camera switch or button on your device. Some laptops have a physical switch that turns the camera on or off. If your laptop has one, make sure it is in the on position before using your camera. Otherwise, please check if your camera has a sliding cover and slide it open."
    Step 4, 5 & 6 — Permissions & VPN Check
    Browser & OS Camera Permissions / Close Secondary Apps
    4. Browser camera permission enabled? • No → Allow camera in browser.
    5. OS camera permission enabled? • No → Enable in OS settings.
    6. VPN or company device? • Yes → Disable VPN or use personal machine.
    "Please make sure no other applications are using your camera, microphone, or screen sharing, and close any unnecessary programs."
    Step 7, 8, 9, 10 & 11 — Rejoin, Reboot & Fallback
    Refresh Browser / Reboot System / 30-Min Policy
    7. Try a different network (switch to hotspot or Wi-Fi).
    8. Ask candidate to rejoin.
    9. Ask candidate to restart device (full reboot resets camera drivers).
    10. Use a different device.
    11. End Goal: Camera issue resolved ✓
    "Please click the refresh button on the top left side of the screen."
    "You will have up to 30 minutes to reconnect. Your previous answers will be saved. If it takes more than 30 minutes, the exam will be automatically submitted. Please contact support@certiverse.com for further assistance if needed."
    🧭 SEB (SECURE EXAM BROWSER) EXAMS

    🔐 Secure Exam Browser — SEB Hardware & Media Diagnostics

    Step 1 — Top-Left SEB Refresh
    Refresh Safe Exam Browser Navigation
    Instruct candidate to click the SEB refresh icon located in the upper-left corner of the window.
    "To refresh Safe Exam Browser, please click the refresh icon located in the upper-left corner of the screen."
    Step 2 — SEB Input Device Switching
    Re-select Audio/Video Devices in SEB Bar
    Refresh SEB media selector dialog and choose an alternate active webcam or microphone device.
    "Please try to refresh the SEB and choose a different audio/video input."
    Step 3 — Bottom-Right Power Icon Exit
    Exit SEB Application to Reset Environment
    Instruct candidate to exit SEB by clicking the bottom-right power icon, fix device settings, and re-enter.
    "To exit Safe Exam Browser, please click the power icon located in the bottom-right corner of the screen."
    Step 4 — SEB Re-entry & Support Escalation
    Re-launch SEB or Escalate to Support
    Re-launch SEB session. If version error persists, refer candidate to support@certiverse.com.
    "There appears to be a problem with your SEB version. Please contact support@certiverse.com to resolve this and continue with the exam."

    ❓ General Booking & Exam Launch

    How can candidates verify if their device meets hardware requirements?
    Candidates should run the compatibility check tool prior to exam day. Note that Chromebooks are not supported.
    "Please verify that your computer meets all system requirements by using the pre-exam compatibility tool on your Certiverse dashboard. Please note that Chromebooks are not supported."
    Blank page on Certiverse.com or launch blocking?
    Corporate VPNs or managed firewalls often block WebRTC streams. Advise switching to a personal machine.
    "Corporate firewalls or VPNs may cause a blank page or stream block. Please switch to a personal computer and personal network connection to access your exam."

    🪪 Identity Verification & Stripe

    Candidate Failed ID Verification Flow
    Support teams perform manual review. Candidates can retry until reservation expiry.
    "Your ID verification attempt will be manually reviewed by our support team. You may retry the verification process via your reservation dashboard until your exam session expires."

    ⏱️ Exam Disconnections & Results

    What happens if disconnected mid-exam?
    Progress auto-saves. Candidate has 30 minutes to reconnect before auto-submission.
    "If you get disconnected, your progress is automatically saved. You have up to 30 minutes to reconnect before your session is auto-submitted."
    When will exam results and score reports be delivered?
    Score validation takes up to 72 hours. Official score reports and badges are sent by email.
    "After completing your exam, answer validation takes up to 72 hours. Your score report, certificate, and digital badge will be delivered via email."
